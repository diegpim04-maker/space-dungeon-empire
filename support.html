How is my order shipped?
All physical goods are dispatched via the Global Fulfillment Network. Shipping typically takes 7-14 business days depending on your sector. Tracking IDs are provided once the tunnel is established.
How do I access digital blueprints?
Once payment is decrypted, an encrypted access link is sent to your registered email. Check your spam folders for transmissions from SPACE_DUNGEON_OS.
Is the checkout secure?
Affirmative. We use Stripe Managed Payments with AES-256 encryption. Your financial data is never stored on Space Dungeon servers.
What if my hardware arrives damaged?
Initiate a [SYSTEM_RECOVERY] request within 48 hours of transit completion. We offer direct replacements for structural failures in physical assets.
Contacting Human Command?
For critical failures, transmit a message to support@dungeon-tech.com. Standard response time: 24h.
